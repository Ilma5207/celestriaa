Our Story

Crafted for the Curious Romantic

Celestria was born from a desire to create a space that feels like magic — open-air skies above, dimly lit tables below, and a menu that excites the explorer within.

We believe food should be fun, fearless, and full of love — that's why we serve globally-inspired dishes with a hint of the fusion. From fusion pizzas with Indian soul to handmade baos, buns, panini breads and elevated burgers — each dish is a blend of creativity, comfort, and craft.

Celestria is a celebration of flavor, friendship, and fantasy — for those who love to try something interesting, together.

Ambience & Experience

  • Open-Air Dining: A roofless haven under the stars.
  • Fountain Courtyard: A tranquil touch to your evening.
  • Dim Romantic Lighting: Vintage charm meets modern glow.
  • Fine Dining Service: Elegant yet relaxed.
  • For Everyone: Couples, friends, families, and travelers alike.
